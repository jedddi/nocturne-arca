Client · Concierge

One quiet point of contact.

Every client is looked after by a single member of the Maison — for acquisitions, for service, and for everything an instrument needs across its life. No queue, no ticket number, no script.

Account Service Repair Atelier visits
01 — Account

Your register with the house.

Your account is the record of what you own: edition numbers, certificates, service history, and the standing of any reservation. It is held quietly by the Maison — there is no password to forget and no public sign-up. An account is opened for you the moment your first reservation is confirmed.

Need a certificate reissued, a delivery address changed, or a summary of what you hold? Write to the Maison and your concierge will see to it — usually within forty-eight hours.

Contact the concierge →
02 — Service

Maintained for decades.

A Nocturne instrument is built to be kept. Routine service — regulation, refinishing, seals, and software where relevant — is carried out at the bench in Le Locle by the same makers who finished the piece.

The first service is complimentary within the first two years of ownership.

Arrange a service →
03 — Repair

Restored, never discarded.

Accidents happen. We repair rather than replace, and we keep the parts to do so for the life of every edition. A repaired instrument returns with its original number and a note in its service history.

Request a repair →
04 — Atelier visits

A morning in Le Locle.

Clients are welcome at the atelier by appointment. Visits are unhurried — a look at the bench, a fitting if you are reserving, and a coffee. The atelier is small, so we host only a few each week.

Plan a visit →
