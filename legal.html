Privacy policy

Last updated · MMXXVI

Maison Nocturne collects only what it needs to correspond with you and to look after the instruments you own. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

What we collect

  • Details you give us directly — your name, email, and the contents of any message or reservation request.
  • Records tied to ownership — edition numbers, certificates, and service history.
  • Basic, non-identifying analytics about how the site is used.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, and — only if you confirm — to send occasional notes from the Maison.
  • To fulfil and service reservations and purchases.

Your rights

You may ask us to show, correct, or delete the data we hold about you at any time, and withdraw consent for correspondence. Write to the Maison and we will action it promptly.

02 — Document

Terms of sale

Last updated · MMXXVI

Reservations

Placing a reservation registers your interest in a numbered edition. It is not a charge. No payment is taken until the instrument ships. A member of the Maison confirms availability and the edition number before any sum is due.

Editions & pricing

Editions are limited and, once closed, are not reopened. Prices are shown in euro and include hand-finishing and the atelier certificate. Shipping and any duties are confirmed at the time of dispatch.

Cancellation & returns

You may cancel a reservation at any time before shipment at no cost. Delivered instruments may be returned within 14 days in their original condition. Hand-personalised pieces are excepted, as noted at the time of order.

Warranty

Each instrument is warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for two years. Service and repair beyond the warranty are arranged through the concierge.

03 — Document

Cookie policy

Last updated · MMXXVI

We keep cookies to a minimum. The Maison uses only what is required for the site to work and to understand, in aggregate, how it is used.

What we set

  • Essential — remember your session and preferences so the site functions. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics — anonymous, aggregate usage. No advertising or cross-site tracking.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may affect how parts of the site behave. Questions may be sent to the Maison.
