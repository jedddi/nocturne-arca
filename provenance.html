Provenance · Le Locle

Where each piece begins.

Every Nocturne instrument carries a record of its making — the materials it is cut from, the hands that finished it, and the numbered certificate it leaves the atelier with.

01 — Origin

Made in the Swiss Jura.

Both instruments are assembled in our atelier in Le Locle. We do not outsource finishing, and we do not assemble to a quota. A piece is completed when the maker is unwilling to improve it further — which is also why editions are small.

Ætheŕ is numbered to 500. Volta is numbered to 300. When an edition closes, it is not reopened.

02 — Materials

Cut from honest stock.

We choose materials that age rather than wear: alloys that take a hand finish, glass ground in-house, and movements regulated on the bench. Nothing is chosen for how it photographs; everything is chosen for how it holds up over a decade.

  • Case & frameCold-forged grade-5 titanium
  • OpticsIn-house ground mineral glass
  • FinishHand-brushed, bead-blasted by maker
  • Movement / moduleBench-regulated, sealed in Le Locle
03 — The makers

A few hands.

The bench is deliberately small. A handful of makers — a watchmaker, an optical technician, a finisher — see every instrument through from raw stock to the certificate. Fewer hands mean fewer compromises, and each piece carries the mark of the person who completed it.

No one assembles to a quota. The same makers who finish a piece are the ones who service it years later, through the concierge.

04 — A timeline

Slowly, on purpose.

The Maison is young and intends to age well. The early record, plainly kept:

05 — The Certificate

A document, not a receipt.

Each instrument ships with a certificate from the atelier — the maker's mark, the edition number, and the date it left the bench. It is the piece's provenance in writing, and it travels with the object for the rest of its life.

06 — After purchase

Kept, not replaced.

Provenance does not end at delivery. Service, repair, and atelier visits are arranged for the life of the instrument through the concierge, so a Nocturne piece can be kept rather than replaced.
